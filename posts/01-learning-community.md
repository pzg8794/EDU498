Hi everyone,

My name is Piter García. I'm originally from the Dominican Republic and moved to the U.S. at 18, an experience that continues to shape how I think about language, identity, belonging, and education. I am a first-generation graduate student working toward initial certification in K–12 Computer Science at Warner while also bringing a background in data science, engineering, and research.

My broader equity work sits at the intersection of education, disability, culturally and linguistically diverse communities, and fairness-aware technology. In my research and future PhD work, I am interested in how data systems, diagnostic systems, and educational systems can either misread people or be redesigned to recognize human complexity more accurately. That question also shapes how I think about teaching: students should not have to fit one narrow mold to be seen as capable.

My teaching placement has been at Pine Brook Elementary in the IGNITE program, where students work with coding, robotics, digital literacy, design, AI, and creative problem-solving. That placement has pushed me to think about literacy beyond print. Students are constantly reading and composing through icons, interfaces, code blocks, images, collaboration, oral explanation, and digital tools.

I am taking this course because I want to better understand how literacy instruction can support students whose strengths are often missed by traditional school structures, especially neurodivergent students, CLD students, and students with multiple intersecting identities. I hope to learn how to design literacy experiences that are rigorous, joyful, culturally responsive, and honest about power.

What I bring to our learning community is an interdisciplinary perspective and a strong commitment to inclusion. I care deeply about classrooms where students do not have to hide parts of themselves to be seen as capable. I am excited to learn with everyone and to keep thinking about how literacy can make students' identities, thinking, and creativity more visible.
