# EDU498 Writing Style Guidelines
## For All Class Submissions, Journals, and Activities

These rules apply to all writing produced for EDU498, including wellness check-ins, freewrites, journal entries, activity responses, and discussion board posts.

---

## Rules

### 1. No em dashes
Do not use — anywhere. Replace with a period, a comma, or restructure the sentence.

**Not this:** Literacy is not a skill — it is a practice.
**This:** Literacy is a practice.

---

### 2. No negative framing
Do not define things by what they are not. State directly what something IS.

**Not this:** Your identity is not a barrier to literacy, it is the key.
**This:** Your identity is the key to literacy.

**Not this:** Joy is not performance, it is pursuit.
**This:** Joy is pursuit.

---

### 3. Keep it concise
Say it in the fewest words possible. No padding, no over-explanation.

---

### 4. Keep it personal
Write in first person. Use "I" statements. Ground everything in lived experience.

---

### 5. Simple grammar
Short sentences. Active voice. No complex nested clauses. If a sentence needs to be read twice to understand, rewrite it.

---

## Quick Checklist Before Submitting
- [ ] No — anywhere in the text
- [ ] No sentences that say what something is NOT
- [ ] Every claim is stated directly and positively
- [ ] Written in first person
- [ ] Short, clear sentences throughout
