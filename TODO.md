### 1. Literacy Narrative
**Points:** 10  
**File:** [`assignments/Assignment_ Literacy Narrative_.docx`](assignments/Assignment_%20Literacy%20Narrative_.docx)  
**Max length:** 5 pages  
**Status:** 🔲 Not started

**Requirements:**
- [ ] **Part 1 — Personal Literacy Autobiography** (1–2 paragraphs): how/when you learned to read and write; challenges and milestones; how you view literacy's role in your life now
- [ ] **Part 2 — Community Exploration**: interview a family member, elder, or community leader; explore socio-cultural factors (migration, technology, political events); compare their journey to yours
- [ ] **Part 3 — Reflection**: literacy as a social practice; how personal and community narratives shape your understanding

**Strategy / Cross-Repo Connections:**
- Your immigration story (Dominican Republic → U.S. at 18) is the core of Part 1 — language shift, identity, belonging
- Community interview: consider a family member whose literacy journey involved a different language, country, or technology context
- Part 3 reflection can draw directly from your 3-2-1 posts: Mackey's "who reads what, in what formats, and why" + Bishop's mirrors/windows + access barrier vs. thinking barrier
- Placement transcripts in [`teaching-placement`](https://github.com/pzg8794/teaching-placement/tree/teaching_placement_shared/transcripts) can provide classroom evidence for how you currently practice and observe literacy
- This narrative will also feed directly into the **Literacy as Power** and **Mini-Unit** assignments below — draft it with that in mind
