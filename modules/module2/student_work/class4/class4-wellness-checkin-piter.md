## Prompt
*What part of yourself has been growing, healing, or changing recently, even if others cannot yet see it?*

---

## Response

Something that has been growing in me, even when others cannot see it, is my relationship with my own mind/brain. I own multiple identities, each is unique and very difficult to spot even for those that know me best. I am neurodivergent, chronically ill, carrying multiple identities: Dominican, bilingual, educator, researcher, coder. For a long time, those identities did not feel like they belonged in the same room together, let alone in a classroom. What has been changing is that I am starting to design spaces; through code, through research, through reflective writing, where all of them can show up at once. My EQUITAS work, quantum computing projects, trauma-informed teaching reflections: these are the same ongoing act of building literacy practices that recognize who I actually am. That recognition, the kind that does not require me to flatten myself to be taken seriously, is what has been quietly growing. I am not just learning to teach. I am learning that my way of thinking, moving through the world, and making meaning is legitimate knowledge.
