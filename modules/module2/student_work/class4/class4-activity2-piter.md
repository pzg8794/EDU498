## Discussion Board Prompt Selected

There is decades of evidence that our current education system is not supporting our marginalized students and instead fostering a White-dominant ideology, connecting to this week's readings, how can we improve the education system to fit the needs of 21st-century students?

---

## How Can It Transform Current Educational Approaches?

The education system was not designed for all students. It was designed around a White-dominant ideology that determined whose language counts, whose history matters, whose way of thinking is intelligent, and whose identity is an asset rather than an obstacle. The structural demand is: design the institution around belonging first, and watch what students are capable of when they no longer have to spend energy proving they deserve to be there.
