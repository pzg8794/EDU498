### **Assignment: Literacy Narrative**

### **10 possible points  *(Last Revised January 2026\)***

### **Objective:** 

This assignment invites students to explore their personal and community literacy experiences. The literacy narrative encourages students to reflect on their own literacy journey, comparing it with the experiences of a community member, while also considering literacy’s broader societal implications, fostering growth and critical thinking through reflection.  
**Total length not to exceed 5 pages** 

### **Instructions:**

* **Personal Literacy Autobiography:** Write a short narrative (1-2 paragraphs) about your personal literacy journey.  
  * How and when did you first learn to read and write?  
  * What significant challenges or milestones shaped your literacy development?  
  * How do you currently view literacy’s role in your life?  
* **Community Exploration:** Interview a family member, elder, or community leader about their literacy experiences.  
  * What were their foundational literacy experiences?  
  * How did socio-cultural factors (e.g., migration, technology, political events) impact their literacy journey?  
  * Compare your literacy journey with theirs.  
* **Reflection:** Provide initial thoughts on literacy as a social practice. How do personal and community narratives shape your understanding of literacy’s role in society?
