# Muhammad (2020): Chapter 2

## Readable Themed Overview

Muhammad's Chapter 2 deepens the idea that literacy instruction should develop more than isolated skills. Literacy should build identity, skills, intellect, criticality, and joy together. When one of those parts is missing, students may complete tasks without experiencing literacy as meaningful or connected to their lives.

Through the Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS lens, this source is important because it gives us a framework for designing instruction that recognizes whole students. Students need chances to know themselves, develop tools, think deeply, question the world, and experience beauty and possibility in learning.

## Teaching Takeaway

Plan literacy lessons with identity, skill, intellect, criticality, and joy together. Do not treat joy or identity as extras after the academic work is done.
