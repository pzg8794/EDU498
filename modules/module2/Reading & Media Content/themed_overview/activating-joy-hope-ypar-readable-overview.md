# Activating Joy and Hope in Critical Literacies Instruction

## Readable Themed Overview

This source connects joy and hope to youth participatory action research. It shows that critical literacy does not have to leave students in skepticism or frustration. When students investigate real issues and create responses, literacy can become a path toward agency.

Through the Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS lens, this matters because students need more than awareness of problems. They need opportunities to question, research, speak, create, and imagine change. Joy and hope become part of critical work when students see that their voices can matter.

## Teaching Takeaway

Use critical literacy projects that let students investigate real questions and create meaningful responses. Pair critique with possibility.
