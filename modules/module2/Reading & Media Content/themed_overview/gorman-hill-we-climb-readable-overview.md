# Amanda Gorman (2021): The Hill We Climb

## Readable Themed Overview

Amanda Gorman's poem shows literacy as public voice, civic imagination, memory, and hope. The poem is not only a performance; it is a multimodal literacy event where language, rhythm, history, body, audience, and national context come together to make meaning.

Through the Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS lens, this source matters because poetry can give students a way to speak about identity, struggle, possibility, and community without reducing literacy to traditional essay writing. It also shows that language can carry critique and hope at the same time.

## Teaching Takeaway

Use poetry and performance as serious literacy. Students can analyze language, voice, audience, history, and hope while also creating their own spoken, written, visual, or multimodal responses.
