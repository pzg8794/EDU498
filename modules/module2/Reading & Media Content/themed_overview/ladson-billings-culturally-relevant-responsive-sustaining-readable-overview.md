# Ladson-Billings (2021): Culturally Relevant, Responsive, and Sustaining Pedagogy

## Readable Themed Overview

Ladson-Billings helps Module 2 understand culturally relevant, responsive, and sustaining pedagogy as more than a teaching style. It is a way of designing learning that takes students' cultures, histories, identities, and communities seriously. The goal is not to add culture as decoration, but to build classrooms where students' knowledge and ways of being are treated as intellectually valuable.

Through the Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS lens, this source matters because students are often asked to succeed by becoming less connected to who they are. Culturally relevant and sustaining pedagogy refuses that tradeoff. It asks teachers to support academic growth while also affirming identity and developing critical understanding of the world.

## Teaching Takeaway

Do not treat culture as an occasional theme. Build lessons where students' identities, communities, languages, and histories shape the work itself.
