# Sealey-Ruiz (2022): Truth, Love & Racial Literacy

## Readable Themed Overview

Sealey-Ruiz connects racial literacy to truth, love, reflection, and responsibility. The source helps Module 2 understand that culturally responsive teaching is not only about selecting diverse texts. It also requires teachers and students to examine how race, identity, history, language, and power shape what happens in classrooms.

Through the Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS lens, this matters because students cannot experience literacy as liberating if classrooms avoid truth. Love is not softness or avoidance; it is the commitment to see students fully, name harm honestly, and build learning spaces where dignity and accountability can exist together.

## Teaching Takeaway

Teach racial literacy with honesty and care. Invite students to read texts, systems, histories, and identities critically while building classroom relationships strong enough to hold that work.
