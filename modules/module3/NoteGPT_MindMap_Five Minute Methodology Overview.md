# Adventist Data Platform and Rating System

## 📊 Overview of the Platform

Interactive visualization mapping thousands of news sources rated for reliability and bias.

Focus on web-based news sources including traditional news, blogs, and fake news sites.

Covers a wide spectrum of information sources people use beyond traditional media outlets.

## 🔍 Reliability Ratings

### Reliability score ranges:Above 40: Highly reliable (fact-based, investigative reporting).24 to 40: Mixed reliability including analysis and opinion content.16 to 24: Problematic, potentially misleading but not outright false.Below 16: Misleading or inaccurate content.

Above 40: Highly reliable (fact-based, investigative reporting).

24 to 40: Mixed reliability including analysis and opinion content.

16 to 24: Problematic, potentially misleading but not outright false.

Below 16: Misleading or inaccurate content.

Differentiates factual reporting from opinion and biased analysis.

## ⚖️ Bias Measurement

Bias is rated on a left-to-right political spectrum.

The middle is not necessarily best—can represent balance or minimal bias.

Extreme left or right bias correlates with lower reliability.

Liberal and conservative perspectives are both valuable for societal discourse but increased extremity correlates with misinformation risk.

## 📰 Examples of Media Source Ratings

New York Times: Left-leaning, reliable reporting mixed with opinion content.

Wall Street Journal: Right-leaning with a similar mixture.

Highly partisan outlets tend to be favored by one side and disliked by the other, with varying reliability.

Extremist sources on both ends of the spectrum are generally less reliable.

## 👩‍💻 Rating Process and Methodology

Articles and episodes are manually rated by panels of trained analysts.

Analysts undergo 30 hours of initial training and 40 hours annual retraining.

Each content piece is independently scored and discrepancies discussed collaboratively.

Uses a tool called Cart (Content Analysis Rating Tool) for scoring.

Over 53,000 articles and episodes rated to date, contributing to a large machine learning training dataset.
