# Class 8 Wellness Check-In — Love and Resilience

**Name:** Piter

## Prompt

Share a phrase, image, or thought that represents a recent experience you have had with love and resilience. Connect your shared idea to someone else's as it applies.

## Response

This image expresses how resilience is not always loud or beautiful at first. Sometimes, resilience is simply waking up again when we are in pain, exhausted, depressed, or carrying more than people can see with the help of that internal light that no one can see. Love shows up in the small decision to keep going, to care for ourselves, and to notice even one piece of beauty around us when life feels heavy.

I got inspired by the first post, **"Bloom Where You're Planted."** Both show growth in a place that does not look easy. The flower is not growing in perfect soil; it is growing through pressure, cracks, and hard ground. That feels connected to many aspects of my experience, such as teaching. Teachers often have to keep showing up while carrying stress, tiredness, and personal challenges, but they still create care, safety, and possibility for others.

For me, resilience is not pretending the concrete is not there, but recognizing that something can still bloom through it.

## Key Course Connection

This connects to the Class 8 focus on love, adversity, and disciplinary skills because resilience is not only an individual trait. It is also shaped by the spaces, relationships, and systems that either support people or make survival harder. In teaching, love appears through designing conditions where students can keep growing even when the ground is difficult.
