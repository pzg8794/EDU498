## Why This Pick Is The Strongest Base

This pick best balances what the Class 7 activity asks for right now.

### Joy

The Minecraft work already contains joy through making, debugging, breakthrough, peer teaching, and visible success. Students experience joy when their code works, when a peer helps them understand a puzzle, when they become the helper, and when their digital build becomes something they can point to and explain.

This continues the Class 3 joy commitment: belonging before building. The lesson should begin with shared modeling, a visible success path, and collaborative roles before expecting independent production.

### Identity

Module 4 asks us to treat identity as more than a personal prompt. Identity should shape the task, the roles, the materials, the modes of expression, and the evidence of learning.

Minecraft fits because students can represent identity through a built world, not only through writing. Ethan can be recognized as a coder, builder, storyteller, cultural knowledge holder, outdoor thinker, and community designer. This aligns with Muhammad's identity pursuit and with Moje, Luke, Davies, and Street's point that literacy tasks position students into roles.

### Multimodal And Multiliteracies

The Minecraft base naturally supports multiple literacies:

- coding literacy
- spatial literacy
- visual design
- oral explanation
- peer teaching
- debugging talk
- digital navigation
- storytelling
- reflective writing

This fits EDU498's course theme that literacy is not only reading and writing. Literacy can include coding, building, making, collaboration, visual design, oral language, and self-advocacy.

### Access And EQUITAS

The existing Minecraft unit already notices access barriers: sign-in, menu navigation, reading NPC directions, executive functioning load, and interface friction. That matters because a student should not be misread as weak in computational thinking when the real barrier is the design of the task.

The redesigned lesson can use:

- visible click paths
- Driver/Navigator roles
- identity-safe choice
- planning cards
- oral or visual explanation options
- debugging logs
- peer-helper roles that do not take over the keyboard

### Strong Existing Evidence

This is the most complete existing base. It has:

- a unit snapshot
- a lesson sequence
- a Warner lesson draft
- EDU498 evidence notes
- ED452B inclusive-design work
- EDE498C source mapping

That means the next step is improvement, not invention.

## Why The Other Candidates Were Not Picked First

The circuit/fan/squishy-circuit path is excellent and should remain the main backup. It may even become the best option if the chosen student is Sofia instead of Ethan. Its strength is joy through collective building and embodied science literacy. Its limitation is that the full mini-unit arc is less developed than Minecraft.

The healthcare AI/coding-as-literacy path is intellectually powerful and closely aligned with EQUITAS, neurodivergence, CLD identity, bilingual access, and criticality. Its limitation is timing and complexity. It may be better for a later draft when the activity asks us to bring criticality forward more explicitly.
