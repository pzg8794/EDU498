## My Theme (Final)

**Joy in literacy depends on recognition and access.**

> Joy in literacy depends on recognition and access. Students may already be doing rich literacy work through debugging, designing, explaining, collaborating, and revising, but that joy becomes invisible when schools only count traditional forms of reading and writing. A joyful literacy classroom must be designed so students’ strengths have real pathways in — and so their multimodal thinking is noticed, valued, and documented.

---

## Why This Theme

This theme maps directly to the course framing: *Joyful Literacies: Access, Agency, and Critical Hope.* Access is not just about getting into the room — it is about whether your way of making meaning is legible once you are there. For neurodivergent students, CLD students, and CS learners, joy often exists in non-traditional forms (debugging, designing, explaining, coding) that go unrecognized without intentional institutional design.

## Connection to IGNITE Placement

In my Pine Brook IGNITE placement (Minecraft unit), students showed joy through debugging, design choices, peer explanation, and revision — not through written paragraphs. That joy was real. But it only became educationally meaningful because the structure recognized it. Without that recognition, it disappears from the record entirely.
