# Mackey (2020): Reading Across Formats

## Readable Themed Overview

Mackey helps Module 2 ask what students read, how they read, and why different formats matter. Literacy is not limited to printed school texts. Students make meaning through books, screens, audio, images, digital tools, and multimodal environments.

Through the Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS lens, this source is about access. Some students enter literacy more easily through visual, oral, digital, cultural, or multimodal pathways. When classrooms treat only one format as serious literacy, they can miss the ways students already think and make meaning.

## Teaching Takeaway

Offer students multiple formats for reading and response. Let format become a bridge into meaning, not a barrier to participation.
