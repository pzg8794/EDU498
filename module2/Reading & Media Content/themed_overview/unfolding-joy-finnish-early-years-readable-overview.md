# Unfolding Joy in Young Children's Literacy Practices

## Readable Themed Overview

This source shows joy as something that appears through young children's everyday literacy practices. Joy is not separate from learning; it can unfold through play, language, movement, social interaction, imagination, and shared discovery.

Through the Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS lens, this source matters because children show literacy in many ways before school reduces literacy to correctness. Their gestures, drawings, stories, questions, relationships, and experiments are part of meaning-making.

## Teaching Takeaway

Notice joy as evidence of engagement and meaning. Design literacy spaces where children can play, explore, collaborate, and make sense of the world in more than one mode.
