# Paris & Alim (2014): Culturally Sustaining Pedagogy

## Readable Themed Overview

Paris and Alim ask what schools are trying to sustain when they claim to be culturally responsive. Their work moves beyond simply including culture and asks teachers to protect, value, and extend the languages, practices, histories, and identities students bring.

Through the Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS lens, this matters because students should not have to become less connected to their communities to be seen as successful. Literacy should help students move across contexts while sustaining the cultural and linguistic practices that already carry meaning.

## Teaching Takeaway

Design literacy instruction that sustains students' language, culture, and community knowledge rather than treating them as bridges to leave behind.
