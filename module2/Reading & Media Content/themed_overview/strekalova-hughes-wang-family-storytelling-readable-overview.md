# Strekalova-Hughes & Wang (2019): Family Storytelling

## Readable Themed Overview

Strekalova-Hughes and Wang show family storytelling as a culturally sustaining literacy practice, especially for children from refugee backgrounds. Storytelling carries memory, identity, language, migration history, family knowledge, and belonging. It is not simply personal sharing; it is a way children and families preserve meaning across places and experiences.

Through the Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS lens, this source matters because schools can easily miss the literacies students bring from home. A child’s family stories may hold cultural knowledge, emotional knowledge, multilingual knowledge, and survival knowledge that traditional school literacy does not always recognize.

## Teaching Takeaway

Invite family and community storytelling into literacy instruction through oral, visual, written, multilingual, and digital forms. Students should not have to disconnect from family history to participate in school literacy.
