# Mirrors, Windows, and Sliding Glass Doors Video

## Readable Themed Overview

This short video reinforces Bishop's idea that students need texts that reflect their own lives and open paths into the lives of others. The video makes the metaphor accessible: a mirror affirms identity, a window builds understanding, and a sliding glass door invites participation.

Through the Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS lens, this matters because representation is part of access. Students should not have to search outside school to find stories that honor their culture, language, family, body, or lived experience.

## Teaching Takeaway

Use the mirror-window-door test when selecting texts. Ask whether students can see themselves, understand others, and enter the work as active meaning-makers.
