# Souto-Manning & Yoon (2018): Multiple Literacies Across Social Contexts

## Readable Themed Overview

Souto-Manning and Yoon help Module 2 understand that young children already participate in many literacies across home, school, family, language, play, and community contexts. Literacy is not something children only begin when school recognizes it. They are already reading worlds, relationships, routines, stories, bodies, languages, and expectations.

Through the Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS lens, this source matters because students are often judged by school-based literacy before teachers understand the literacies they already use. CLD students, multilingual students, neurodivergent students, and students with complex life experiences may show meaning-making through oral language, gesture, family storytelling, play, translation, drawing, and social participation.

## Teaching Takeaway

Look for the literacies students already bring. Young children’s language, play, stories, home practices, and social interactions should become part of classroom literacy design.
