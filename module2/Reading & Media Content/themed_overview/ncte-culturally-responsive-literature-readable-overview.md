# NCTE: Culturally Responsive Approaches to Teaching Literature

## Readable Themed Overview

The NCTE practitioner guide helps translate culturally responsive literacy theory into classroom practice. It asks teachers to think carefully about text selection, discussion design, student identity, representation, and how literature can either open or close access.

Through the Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS lens, this source matters because teaching literature is not neutral. The texts teachers choose, the questions they ask, and the forms of response they allow all communicate whose lives and languages belong in the classroom. A culturally responsive approach makes literature a space for identity, criticality, joy, and connection rather than only analysis for a grade.

## Teaching Takeaway

Use literature instruction to affirm identity, expand perspectives, and invite critical response. Students should read texts that matter and respond through discussion, writing, art, performance, digital work, and personal connection.
