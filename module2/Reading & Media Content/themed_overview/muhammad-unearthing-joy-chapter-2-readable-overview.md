# Muhammad (2020): Unearthing Joy, Chapter 2

## Readable Themed Overview

Muhammad's work on joy reframes literacy as more than skill-building. Joy is not a reward after learning; it is part of meaningful learning itself. Students need classrooms where literacy connects to identity, beauty, confidence, curiosity, history, and community.

Through the Puzzle Plan and EQUITAS lens, joy is access. Students who are often misread by school systems should not have to experience literacy only as correction, pressure, compliance, or deficit. Joy helps students feel that learning belongs to them.

## Teaching Takeaway

Design literacy experiences where students can feel ownership, beauty, connection, and possibility. Joy should be planned for, not treated as extra.
