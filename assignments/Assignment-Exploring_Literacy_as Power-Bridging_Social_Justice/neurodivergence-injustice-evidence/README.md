## Evidence Use

Use this evidence to analyze communication design and access barriers. The anger, pain, exhaustion, and disgust in the records are not a problem to erase; they are part of what shows harm. The practical task is to preserve both the emotional truth and the evidence trail so the final argument can push for change.

Useful evidence moves:

- "The email record shows that..."
- "The transcript records a student/team discussion about..."
- "The advisor framed the situation as an accessibility issue that may need formal escalation..."
- "The issue is not only an individual misunderstanding; it is a communication-access design problem."

For formal claims, separate what the record directly shows from what the record helps us infer. That is not about making the tone pretty; it is about making the argument harder to dismiss.
