### **Assignment: Joy Oriented Literacy Pursuit**

*10 possible  points (Last Revised Jan. 2026\)*

### **Objective:**

Students will engage in joy-oriented literacy pursuit. They will create, share, and reflect on a literacy-based project that promotes liberation, self-determination, self-reliance, and self-empowerment, while experiencing the joy, love, and aesthetic fulfillment inherent in literacy practices.

### **Instructions:**

**Select, Create, and Share a Literacy Pursuit**

* **Select:** Choose a literacy pursuit inspired by Muhammad’s framework and aligned with your personal interests.
  * **Examples of Joy-Oriented Literacy Pursuits:**
* Workshop
* Performance
* Cookbooks
* Community Murals
* Minecraft creations
* Music playlists
* Translations ( can be a multi medium endeavor such as music and text)
* Mapping projects
* Poetry
* **Create:** Your literacy pursuit must represent an act of reading, writing, speaking, or art  that embodies greater goals such as liberation, self-determination, and empowerment.
  * You can collaborate with others, remix existing content, and/or  make your literacy pursuit participatory ( have others engage in its creation). HITRECORD
* **Share**: The final product should be shared with an **authentic audience** ( this may be in a classroom setting,  on a YouTube channel, a social media platform, or submitted to a publication/blog) and you have interacted with your audience's feedback.

**Reflect on the Experience**
Write a 2 \-4 page reflection ( double spaced) or create an equivalent multimodal reflection (e.g., a presentation, video, or audio recording 5-7 min MAX) that addresses the following questions:

1. **Product:** What did you create, and who did you share it with? **(Provide links, attachments, pictures/ screenshots as appropriate.)**
2. **Process:** What was your creative and learning process? (Include resources used, steps taken, etc.)
3. **Specify:** How did your literacy pursuit cultivate joy, love, and/or aesthetic fulfillment for you?
4. **Connect to Text(s):** How did your creation exemplify Muhammad’s ideas of a literacy pursuit? Did you connect the experience to any other course readings?
5. **Connect to Practice:** How might this experience inform your future teaching practice? How could you incorporate this or similar literacy pursuits into your classroom?
